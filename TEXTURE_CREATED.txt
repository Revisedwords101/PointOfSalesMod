================================================================================
🎮 HOW TO USE:
================================================================================

The texture is now automatically used by your VendingMachineScreen class!

In VendingMachineScreen.java, it's referenced like this:

    private static final ResourceLocation TEXTURE =
            ResourceLocation.fromNamespaceAndPath("posdelight", "textures/gui/vending_machine.png");

When the screen opens, it will render this background texture.

================================================================================
🎨 CUSTOMIZING THE TEXTURE:
================================================================================

If you want to create a more custom design:

1. Open the PNG in an image editor (Paint.NET, Photoshop, GIMP)
2. Add your own colors, patterns, or designs
3. Save and rebuild your mod with: ./gradlew build --no-daemon

For inspiration, you can look at existing Minecraft GUI textures like:
- Chest texture
- Furnace texture  
- Crafting table texture
